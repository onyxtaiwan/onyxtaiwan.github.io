How it works

  1. Step 1

    Book online

    Pay the balance in cash (TWD) to your chauffeur at the end of the ride.

Questions

How do I find my chauffeur?+

The day before, we add you to a WhatsApp group with your chauffeur. Your chauffeur tracks your flight and tells you in the group where to meet after you land. You can add a meet & greet name sign for TWD 300 (pick-ups 05:00–22:00).

What if my flight is delayed?+

We track your flight, so a delay costs you nothing. Free waiting is 60 minutes after landing (15 minutes from your pick-up time if you choose your own time). After that, overtime is charged by the hour.

Is the price fixed?+

Yes. The price in the booking form includes tolls, parking and fuel. Late night (22:00–06:00) is TWD 200 extra, and child seats are TWD 200 each.

How do I pay?+

Book online and we'll send your deposit link by email and WhatsApp. The balance is paid in cash (TWD) to your chauffeur.

How much luggage fits?+

Standard and Premium Car: up to 3 × 28-inch suitcases. Premium Van: up to 8. Luxury: up to 6. Two 20-inch suitcases count as one 28-inch.

Do you also drive to the airport?+

Yes. Choose Departure in the booking form. Plan to arrive at the airport 3 hours before an international flight.
