Taichung & Gaomei Wetlands Day Tour

Instagram Hotspots: Taichung City & Gaomei Wetlands

Taichung's most photogenic spots in one day, ending with sunset over the Gaomei Wetlands. Best from a Taichung hotel; from Taipei, choose 12 hours or split it over 2 days.

The route

Designed for photography lovers. Connects the colorful Rainbow Village, a storybook-style ice cream shop, creative markets, and architectural masterpieces, concluding with a spectacular sunset at the wetlands.
