Cancellation (deposit)

  • 7 days (168 hours) or more before pick-up: full deposit refund.
  • 3 to 7 days (72 to 168 hours) before pick-up: 50% of the deposit refunded.
  • Less than 72 hours before pick-up, or no-show: deposit not refunded.
  • Refunds are made in TWD to your original PayPal account. Exchange-rate differences are not covered.

Changes & no-shows

  • Free changes to date, time or flight up to 48 hours before pick-up. A new route is re-quoted.
  • Arrivals: if we can't reach you 60 minutes after landing, the booking counts as a no-show.
  • Short-notice bookings (48–72 hours) are confirmed once a vehicle is available. If none is available, your deposit is fully refunded.
  • If we can't confirm your booking, your deposit is fully refunded.

On the day

  • Balance is paid in cash (TWD) to your chauffeur.
  • Free waiting: 60 minutes after landing for arrivals, 15 minutes for other pick-ups. After that, overtime is charged per 30 minutes. Flight delays are not charged.
  • Stops added on the day: TWD 500 each plus TWD 25 per extra km.
  • Cleaning fee for vomiting or smoking: TWD 2,000. Pets must travel in a carrier and be declared in advance.
  • Children under 4 must use a child seat (Taiwan law). A stroller or foldable wheelchair counts as one 28-inch bag.
  • Prices are charged in TWD. Converted prices are for reference only. Lunar New Year (Feb 3–11, 2027): +50%.

Force Majeure (Weather & Flight Disruptions)

In the event of flight cancellations due to severe weather (e.g., typhoons) or other force majeure events, please contact us immediately. We will offer a free rescheduling or a full refund upon verification of the official flight cancellation status.

How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel your booking or request a refund, please contact us directly via Email or WhatsApp. Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 14-28 business days.
