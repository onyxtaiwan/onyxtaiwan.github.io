Get Your Price & Book

Cancellation (deposit)

  • 7 days (168 hours) or more before pick-up: full deposit refund.
  • 3 to 7 days (72 to 168 hours) before pick-up: 50% of the deposit refunded.
  • Less than 72 hours before pick-up, or no-show: deposit not refunded.
  • Please give the exact number of passengers, children and suitcases. If the details are wrong and everyone can't fit in the car on the day, the booking is non-refundable.
  • Refunds are made in TWD to your original payment method. Exchange-rate differences are not covered.

Changes & no-shows

  • Free changes to date, time or flight up to 48 hours before pick-up. A new route is re-quoted.
  • Arrivals: if we can't reach you 60 minutes after landing (or 15 minutes after the pick-up time you chose), the booking counts as a no-show.
  • Short-notice bookings (48–72 hours) are confirmed once a vehicle is available. If none is available, your deposit is fully refunded.
  • If we can't confirm your booking, your deposit is fully refunded.

On the day

  • Balance is paid in cash (TWD) to your chauffeur.
  • Free waiting: 60 minutes after landing for arrivals (15 minutes from your pick-up time if you choose your own time), 15 minutes for other pick-ups. After that, overtime is charged by the hour. Flight delays are not charged.
  • Transfers: stops added on the day are TWD 500 each plus TWD 25 per extra km. Charters: changes on the day may add a mileage & mountain surcharge.
  • Transfer stops: up to 15 minutes each. For longer visits, please book a charter.
  • Charters: overtime is charged after the hours shown in your booking. Days with a mountain fee include 1 extra hour.
  • Cleaning fee for vomiting or smoking: TWD 2,000. Pets must travel in a carrier and be declared in advance.
  • Children aged 0–4 must use a child seat (Taiwan law). A stroller counts as 2 × 28-inch suitcases, a wheelchair as 3; both need a Premium Van.
  • Prices are charged in TWD. Converted prices are for reference only. Lunar New Year (Feb 3–11, 2027): +50%.

Force Majeure (Weather & Flight Disruptions)

In the event of flight cancellations due to severe weather (e.g., typhoons) or other force majeure events, please contact us immediately. We will offer a free rescheduling or a full refund upon verification of the official flight cancellation status.

How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel your booking or request a refund, please contact us directly via Email or WhatsApp. Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 14-28 business days.

Questions

How do I book?+

Fill in the booking form and you'll see your price right away. We check your trip and send your deposit link by email and WhatsApp. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is paid.

How do I pay?+

Pay the deposit online with the link we send you. The balance is paid in cash (TWD) to your chauffeur.

What if my flight is delayed?+

We track your flight, so delays are not charged. Free waiting is 60 minutes after landing (15 minutes from your pick-up time if you choose your own time). After that, overtime is charged by the hour.

What's included in the price?+

The car, chauffeur, fuel, tolls and parking. Overtime, entrance tickets and meals are not included.

Do you have child seats?+

Yes: rear-facing seats (under 2) and child seats (ages 2–4). Taiwan law requires one for children aged 0–4. TWD 200 each on transfers, free on charters. Add them in the booking form.

Do your chauffeurs speak English?+

Our chauffeurs speak basic English and use a translation app, and we answer messages in English. A fluent-English chauffeur is available on request on charters for TWD 1,000 per day.

How much luggage fits in each car?+

Standard and Premium Car: up to 3 × 28-inch suitcases. Premium Van: up to 8. Luxury: up to 6. Two 20-inch suitcases count as one 28-inch.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Cancel 7 days or more before pick-up for a full deposit refund, or 3 to 7 days before for 50%. Less than 72 hours before: the deposit is not refunded. See the Booking Terms.
